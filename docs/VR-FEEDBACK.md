# Whereabouts VR Feedback Checklist

> **EXPERIMENTAL SKYRIM VR BUILD — UNTESTED IN-GAME**

Please report the exact Skyrim VR, SKSEVR, VR Address Library, Skyrim VR ESL Support, SKSE Menu Framework, Engine Fixes VR, and Whereabouts versions you used, plus your headset/controller type.

Test on a disposable profile and save, then report each item separately:

- Menu opening, closing, page navigation, cursor, and controller interaction.
- NPC search and selection, including console and crosshair selection where available.
- At least one non-destructive command such as Select in Console or Inventory.
- Tracking creation/removal and marker behavior.
- A save/load cycle with a tracked or favorited NPC.
- Normal reopening of Whereabouts after the save/load cycle.

Include `Whereabouts.log`, SKSE logs, the exact action that failed, and whether the problem reproduces. Do not use a valuable save for first testing.

VR can move into a future main release only after three independent Skyrim VR users confirm all six areas and no reproducible startup crash, save/load failure, marker corruption, or core menu/input failure remains. Until then, the candidate stays separate, experimental, and tool-validated only.
